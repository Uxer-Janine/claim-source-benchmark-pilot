# Independent claim–source review packet

Use this packet to make your own assessment or invite someone to review a case. Organizers should share this file by itself before sharing the repository, game, or existing assessments. It contains claim summaries, citations, and recorded passage locations. The locations are starting points and can influence which evidence you notice. Follow the source beyond the locator when relevant. The original Wikipedia revisions are unrecorded.

## Instructions

1. Choose a case. Read the claim summary and cited source before looking for other assessments. Record what you could access and whether you had already seen a human or AI judgment. Use **unknown** if unsure.
2. Separate details that can be checked independently. Record what the source establishes for each, where you found it, and what remains uncertain. Keep factual truth and support from this citation separate.
3. Use the verdict key below. If you cannot access enough material to judge, describe the limitation and leave the verdict unset.
4. Save your response before discussing the case with others. Use a stable reviewer identifier and share through the organizer’s agreed channel. Include citations and locators. Keep private details and quotations with unresolved reuse rights out of your submission.

| Verdict | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Supported | The source establishes the statement and its important details. |
| Partially supported | It establishes part of the statement or a weaker version. |
| Compatible but unconfirmed | It supports a cautious inference without confirming the statement. |
| Not supported | It gives no basis for the statement and no directly conflicting evidence. |
| Contradicted | It directly conflicts with the statement. |

If all subclaims have judgments, record the overall result in this order. All Supported gives Supported. Otherwise any Supported or Partially supported gives Partially supported. Otherwise any Compatible but unconfirmed gives Compatible but unconfirmed. Otherwise any Contradicted gives Contradicted. Otherwise use Not supported. Flag any contradicted subclaim separately even when the overall label differs. If you cannot complete the check, explain the gap and preserve any provisional notes separately.

## Assessment template

- **Case ID, reviewer identifier, review date**
- **Prior exposure:** Had you seen another human assessment? Had you seen an AI assessment? Answer yes, no, or unknown for each.
- **Source access:** Full, partial, unavailable, or unknown. Give the link or version consulted and say what you could read.
- **Evidence locations:** Give page, section, paragraph, line, or stable fragment where available. Note other evidence you checked.
- **Proposed subclaims:** List each checkable detail. For each, give the verdict if assessable, evidence location, and reason. Flag any contradiction explicitly.
- **Overall verdict and reasoning:** Explain how the details contribute. Leave the verdict unset if you cannot judge.
- **Confidence and uncertainty:** High means you expect another assessor to reach the same conclusion. Medium allows a reasonable difference in interpretation. Low reflects unclear or incomplete evidence. Say what could change your assessment.
