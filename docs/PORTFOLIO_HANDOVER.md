# Portfolio and Outreachy handover

## Problem and motivation

A Wikipedia sentence can sound plausible while its citation supports only part of it. Janine Makorre’s Outreachy contribution exercise surfaced the practical difficulty of separating exact wording, evidence, and inference. Cases such as the surrogacy estimate show why checking the amount alone can miss gaps in who made the estimate and how many clinics the source describes.

## Contribution and method

Janine prepared the source research report and original human assessments, guided the methodology and learning design, and reviewed how the work is presented. AI assistance was used for implementation of the tools and Evidence Desk and for drafting and editing documentation. The historical AI responses are separate research material. The review packet and invitation are prepared for future use.

The pilot checks source support rather than factual truth in general. It records citations, locations, verdicts, reasoning, confidence, and uncertainty. It splits compound claims where useful and keeps later reviews and disagreements separate from the historical judgments. Scoring requires at least two distinct, independently completed, confirmed human assessments and a documented reference decision.

## What exists and what it shows

- **Published library:** five structured case records, an assessment guide, a written lesson, and educational illustrations.
- **Tools:** schema validation and an evaluator with example predictions. The example checks the workflow and produces zero scored comparisons.
- **Published learning experience:** Evidence Desk has one playable case, A04, and four previews. Learners open evidence files, sort claim details, and compare their reasoning with a documented assessment awaiting independent review.
- **Defensible findings:** one citation can support a numerical estimate while leaving attribution or clinic counts unresolved. Historical AI responses illustrate differing readings of evidence and an access-related abstention. The sample cannot establish model performance or learning outcomes.

## Limits and next work

The five original human assessments remain provisional. Exact Wikipedia revisions, some source access, and the original AI inputs and run details remain unresolved. Public evidence is inspectable through citations, locations, and labeled summaries where quotations are withheld.

Invite independent reviews using the standalone packet, recover verified revisions or better locators where possible, and test Evidence Desk with first-time learners and a screen reader. Guided onboarding and more playable cases are future public development. Decide how to treat subclaim disagreements before using compound cases for scored evaluation.
