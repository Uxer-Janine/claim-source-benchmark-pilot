# v0.1.0 — Exploratory five-record pilot

- Includes real cases A01, A03, A04, A08, and B09 from the Claim Verification Report, with original verdicts, subclaim judgments where recorded, and historical AI comparisons.
- Uses labeled public summaries, source citations, and recorded passage locations. Complete transcriptions remain in the private research copy.
